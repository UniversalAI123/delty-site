Questions people ask

Where is my data?

On your iPhone. Delty has no accounts and no server that holds your record. Nothing is uploaded unless you share it yourself (a visit summary, a share card, an archive).

I got a new phone. How do I move my record?

On the old phone: Settings → Your data → create an archive with photos. Send the file to the new phone with AirDrop or Files. On the new phone, open Delty and choose Restore from an archive on the Welcome screen. Settings also has a "Moving to a new phone?" row with these steps.

I lost my phone. Is my record gone?

If you never made an archive, yes — that is the cost of having no cloud copy. Delty keeps a private daily restore point on the phone itself, but not off it. Make an archive occasionally and keep it where you keep your other files.

Does Delty tell me what to take?

No, and it never will. It records what you already do. Dose Check verifies the arithmetic on numbers your clinician gave you and shows every step; it does not suggest a number. If any starting value is a guess, stop and ask the person who prescribed it.

Why can I not send my record to iCloud?

Apple's App Review rules for health apps do not allow personal health information to be stored in iCloud. Archives are the compliant path, and they keep you in control of every copy.

What is free and what is Pro?

Free: unlimited logging, reminders, check-ins, measurements, Apple Health import, archives, Dose Check, and your first visit summary export. Pro: photo comparison at Day 30, 60 and 90, DELTY Replay, unlimited visit summaries and photo shares. One annual plan; cancel anytime in your Apple subscription settings.

I recorded a dose by mistake.

Tap Undo on the confirmation, or open Track and correct today's entry. Undone doses keep a dated receipt so the record stays honest.

Reminders are not arriving.

Reminders are configured in the routine builder; delivery needs iOS notification permission. Settings → Reminders shows both. If notifications are off, Open Settings takes you to the iOS switch.

I want my analytics data deleted.

Analytics are off unless you turned them on, and never contain health data. To delete what was sent, email us with the reference shown in Settings → Privacy.

Something is broken.

Email the steps you took and what you expected. Bugs get fixed in the next update and you will hear back when it ships.
