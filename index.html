Record the routine you already follow

Pens, pills or vials, in your units and on your schedule. One tap from the lock screen. Any day can be corrected; undone doses keep a dated receipt.

2

Note how you feel, when it happens

Energy, sleep and symptoms — including the ones nobody else asks about, like hair shedding or appetite. Logged, counted by week, never interpreted.

3

Bring one page to your appointment

Dated doses, measurements and reported symptoms on a single page. Your first export is free. Comparable photos are the optional layer on top.

Nothing leaves your phone unless you share it. No accounts, no cloud copy of your record, no ad SDKs. Backups are files you control.
