Questions

Straight answers.

Does Delty tell me what to take?

No, and it never will. It records what you already do. Ask your prescriber or pharmacist about amounts and timing.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone. Core tracking needs no account. Purchases and optional services are covered in our privacy policy.

What does it cost?

Delty Pro is $49.99 a year with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers, or $7.99 a week. Apple shows the exact price before you confirm.

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone on iOS 16.4 or later.

I got a new phone. What now?

Make an archive in Settings, move it with AirDrop or Files, then choose Restore from an archive on the welcome screen.

Is this a medical device?

No. Delty is a personal record. It does not diagnose or treat anything. More on the support page.
