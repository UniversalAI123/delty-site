How it works

Three things, every day. None of them take a minute.

01

Record the routine you already follow

Pens, pills or vials, in your units, on your schedule. One tap from the lock screen. Any day can be corrected; undone doses keep a dated receipt.

02

Note how you feel, when it happens

Energy, sleep and symptoms — including the ones nobody else asks about: hair shedding, appetite, injection-site reactions. Logged, counted by week, never interpreted.

03

Bring one page to your appointment

Dated doses, measurements and reported symptoms on a single page a clinician can read in a minute. Your first export is free.

How Delty treats your record

Your health record stays on your phone.

Core tracking needs no account. Your health record stays local; purchases and optional services have separate data flows explained in our privacy policy. Archives are files you control.

We document. We never advise.

Delty does not suggest a substance, an amount or a schedule. Keep the instructions you received alongside your observations and questions for your clinician.

No tricks.

The daily record is free. Pro is one annual plan with a free trial, or weekly with no trial; every price is shown before anything is charged. Cancel anytime in your Apple settings.

Questions

Straight answers.

More on the support page.

Where is my data?

Your health record is stored on your iPhone. Core tracking needs no account. Purchases, opt-in analytics and the restricted research pilot use the services described in our privacy policy.

Does Delty tell me what to take?

No, and it never will. It records what you already do. Keep existing instructions and observations together; ask your prescriber or pharmacist about amounts and administration.

Why no iCloud sync?

Delty does not offer automatic cloud sync for your health record. You can export an archive and choose where to keep it; each exported copy needs its own protection.

I got a new phone.

Create an archive with photos in Settings, send the file to the new phone with AirDrop or Files, then choose Restore from an archive on the Welcome screen.

Is this a medical device?

No. Delty is for personal record-keeping. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Talk to your healthcare provider before making medical decisions.
