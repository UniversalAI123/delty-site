How it works

Three things, every day. None of them take a minute.

Record the routine you already follow

Pens, pills or vials, in your units, on your schedule. One tap from the lock screen. Any day can be corrected; undone doses keep a dated receipt.

Note how you feel, when it happens

Energy, sleep and symptoms — including the ones nobody else asks about: hair shedding, appetite, injection-site reactions. Logged, counted by week, never interpreted.

Bring one page to your appointment

Dated doses, measurements and reported symptoms on a single page a clinician can read in a minute. Your first export is free.

Check-in

Thirty seconds. Future-you reads this.

Energy and sleep on a five-point scale, symptoms as chips, an optional note. Severity and timing if you want them. Nothing is scored, ranked or explained — it is your record, kept the way you would keep it.

  • Partial check-ins save; energy alone is a record.
  • Past days are editable in Backfill, and say so.

Questions

Straight answers.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone. Delty has no accounts and no server that holds your record. Nothing is uploaded unless you share it yourself.

Does Delty tell me what to take?

No, and it never will. It records what you already do. Dose Check verifies arithmetic on numbers your clinician gave you and shows every step; it does not suggest a number.

Why no iCloud sync?

Apple's App Review rules for health apps do not allow personal health information to be stored in iCloud. Archives are the compliant path and keep you in control of every copy.

I got a new phone.

Create an archive with photos in Settings, send the file to the new phone with AirDrop or Files, then choose Restore from an archive on the Welcome screen.

Is this a medical device?

No. Delty is for personal record-keeping. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Talk to your healthcare provider before making medical decisions.
