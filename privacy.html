Privacy Policy

Delty · Universal AI LLC · Effective September 7, 2026

The short version

Your data lives on your device. Delty has no accounts, no server-side database of your logs, and no advertising SDKs. We cannot see your weight, your photos, your routine, or your notes — by design.

What the app stores

Everything you enter — routine items, weights, measurements, check-ins, notes, and progress photos — is stored locally on your device. It is included in your own device backups (e.g., iCloud device backup) under Apple's protections, and never transmitted to us.

What leaves your device

  • Nothing automatically. Sharing a card, exporting a CSV, or exporting a doctor report happens only when you explicitly trigger it, through Apple's share sheet, to a destination you pick.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We receive anonymous transaction status (e.g., "subscription active") from Apple/our billing processor — never payment details.
  • Usage analytics, if enabled in a future version, are limited to anonymous app events (e.g., "onboarding completed"). Health information — weights, compound names, side effects, photos, notes — is never included in analytics. We do not sell data and do not share data with advertising networks.

Camera and photos

Camera and photo-library access are used solely to capture and select your progress photos. Photos stay on your device. Alongside each scan, Delty stores a small analysis image and the capture measurements it uses to check that later scans can be compared (phone angle, the body landmarks you place, and lighting statistics). These stay on your device with the photo.

Motion sensor

While the camera is open, Delty reads your phone’s tilt from the motion sensor to help you hold each photo at the same angle. The tilt values are kept only as part of the scan’s capture measurements on your device. Motion data is never transmitted.

Dose Check

Numbers you enter into Dose Check, and any label photo you attach, are stored only on your device. Delty does not transmit them and does not use them for any purpose other than showing you the arithmetic.

Export my record

You can create an archive of your record — entries and progress photos — from Settings. The archive is created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet; you choose where it goes. Delty does not keep a copy of your record on any server and does not store your record in iCloud. If you later restore an archive on another phone, that happens on that phone.

Apple Health

If you choose to import from Apple Health, Delty reads weight and waist measurements you already recorded there and adds them to your local record. Delty never writes to Apple Health and never transmits Health data.

Usage analytics (optional, off by default)

Delty can send anonymous usage events to PostHog (a third-party analytics provider) only if you turn this on in Settings. Events describe which features were used (for example, that a check-in was saved) with numeric or true/false properties only; they never include weights, measurements, routine names, notes, photos, or anything you typed. A random per-install identifier is used, never your name, email, or device identifier. Turning analytics off stops uploads and clears the local queue and identifier; it cannot recall events already sent. To have previously sent events deleted, contact contact@universalaillc.com.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We run no push-notification server.

Deleting your data

Settings → "Delete all my data" permanently removes everything Delty stores on the device. Deleting the app does the same.

Age

Delty is intended for users 18 and older.

Health disclaimer

Delty is a tracking tool. It is not a medical device and provides no medical or dosing advice. Consult your healthcare provider for medical decisions.
